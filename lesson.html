Core causal lesson

Political stabilization and economic recovery increased European demand for Asian luxuries. Long-distance trade involved transport, intermediaries, tolls, risk and profit. High prices and state competition encouraged investment in maritime alternatives. European voyages entered existing Afro-Eurasian systems and increasingly connected commerce to naval force, territorial claims and colonization.

Exit ticket

  1. Why were Asian luxury goods expensive in Europe?
  2. How could political change alter trade without stopping it?
  3. Why did Portugal and Castile invest in different routes?
  4. Explain the chain from luxury demand to colonial expansion.
