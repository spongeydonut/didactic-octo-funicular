Prototype sourcing rule

Historical events remain anchored to documented chronology. Political borders and the illustrated map are teaching reconstructions, not primary-source cartography. Before classroom-final release, each event and political layer should be verified against specialist historical atlases and scholarly sources.

1453: Ottoman conquest of Constantinople.
1469: Marriage of Isabella of Castile and Ferdinand of Aragon.
1488: Dias rounds the Cape.
1492: Granada falls; Columbus reaches the Caribbean.
1498: da Gama reaches Calicut.
1511: Portuguese conquest of Malacca.
1517: early Portuguese contact with Ming China.
