This prototype separates documented events from student counterfactuals. Geographic city placement uses latitude/longitude and the live map uses OpenStreetMap geographic tiles.

1453 — Constantinople

Ottoman conquest ends Byzantine rule. The lesson explicitly avoids the inaccurate claim that trade simply stopped.

1469 — Isabella & Ferdinand

Dynastic marriage links Castile and Aragon without instantly creating a centralized modern Spain.

1488 — Dias

Dias rounds the Cape of Good Hope.

1492 — Granada & Columbus

Granada falls; Columbus reaches inhabited Caribbean lands while seeking Asia.

1498 — Calicut

Da Gama enters an established Indian Ocean commercial system.

1511 — Malacca

Portuguese conquest changes political control but does not end regional commerce.

1517 — Ming China contact

Portuguese access remains subject to Ming political regulation.

1519 — Magellan

Spanish-sponsored expedition seeks westward access to Asian markets.

Reference families for teacher verification: Encyclopaedia Britannica; Library of Congress; Metropolitan Museum of Art histories of Indian Ocean trade; World Historical Gazetteer; OpenStreetMap geographic data.
