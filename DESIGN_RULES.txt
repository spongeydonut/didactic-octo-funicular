THE SPICE RACE — LOCKED DESIGN RULES
HISTORICAL INTEGRITY: gameplay never overrides established history.
LEARNING: every mechanic and animation serves a historical learning objective.
CONTINUITY: future campaigns extend the same historical world.
CAUSE & EFFECT: every choice shows decision → change → world response → meaning.
MAP TRUTH: real latitude/longitude remains the coordinate truth layer; historical political regions sit above it. Modern borders are not used as the historical lesson layer.
VISUAL LANGUAGE: GOLD historical record; BLUE world simulation; ORANGE student counterfactual.
