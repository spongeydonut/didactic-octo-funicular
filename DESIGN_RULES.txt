THE SPICE RACE — CORE DESIGN RULES
1. Historical Integrity: gameplay never overrides established history; counterfactuals are labeled.
2. Learning: every mechanic and animation serves a historical learning objective.
3. Continuity: campaigns extend one persistent historical world.
4. Cause & Effect: decision -> visible change -> world response -> historical meaning.
5. Geographic Alignment: cities, routes, events, and animations use latitude/longitude on the same map system.
